# Offline lunar atlas

Surface texture: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit (2025 color map), Ernie Wright / NASA GSFC / LROC / Arizona State University.
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/
Original: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/lroc_color_2k.jpg
Downloaded 2026-10-13. Texture is processed for visualization, not quantitative albedo measurements. App renders a monochrome, resolution-limited preview with an approximate smooth-sphere terminator; crater relief/shadows and eclipses are not simulated.
NASA SVS media usage: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/help/

Feature names, coordinates and diameters: IAU/USGS Gazetteer of Planetary Nomenclature. Individual feature records linked from each entry.
https://planetarynames.wr.usgs.gov/SearchResults?Target=16_Moon
Snapshot retrieved 2026-10-13. Positive east longitude, lunar planetographic latitude; spherical reference surface.

Selection informed by the Astronomical League Lunar Observing Program (naked-eye maria and binocular checklist). App observing descriptions are original; instrument suitability is guidance, not certification.
https://www.astroleague.org/lunar-observing-program/

Ephemerides: bundled Astronomy Engine, including IAU lunar rotation model and topocentric observer geometry. Moonlight: Krisciunas & Schaefer (1991), PASP 103, 1033, equations 1–3, 15, 19–21, with distance and near-full opposition correction. Default zenith sky 21.6 V mag/arcsec², extinction 0.172 mag/airmass; no weather measurement, no eclipse correction. Domain caveats displayed in app.
https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1991PASP..103.1033K
