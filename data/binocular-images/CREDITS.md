# StarHop binocular survey illustrations

These small survey cutouts are educational illustrations, not live images or
binocular eyepiece simulations. Their copyrights are separate from StarHop's
MIT software license. Source coordinates, angular widths and original cutout
URLs are recorded in client/src/lib/binocular-media.json in the source repository.

## Optical: files ending in -optical.jpg

Digitized Sky Survey (DSS2), produced by the Space Telescope Science Institute
under US Government grant NAG W-2166. Photographic material comes from the
Palomar and UK Schmidt telescopes. Survey copyright holders include STScI/AURA,
Caltech, UK SERC/PPARC and the Anglo-Australian Observatory. Color mosaics by CDS.
Educational and other noncommercial use is permitted with acknowledgment;
commercial reuse requires permission from the relevant copyright holders.

Policy and copyright details: https://archive.stsci.edu/publishing/data-use
Survey acknowledgment: https://archive.stsci.edu/dss/acknowledging.html

## Near infrared: files ending in -infrared.jpg

Atlas Image mosaics courtesy of 2MASS/UMass/IPAC-Caltech/NASA/NSF. The Two Micron
All Sky Survey is a joint project of the University of Massachusetts and the
Infrared Processing and Analysis Center/California Institute of Technology,
funded by NASA and the National Science Foundation. Color mosaics by CDS.
The Atlas Image mosaics are released into the public domain with acknowledgment
requested. Colors represent J/H/K near-infrared bands, not visual colors.

Policy: https://www.ipac.caltech.edu/2mass/gallery/aboutimages.html

## Cutout service

This work uses HiPS2FITS, a service provided by CDS, Strasbourg Astronomical
Observatory, France: https://alasky.cds.unistra.fr/hips-image-services/hips2fits

Surveys: CDS/P/DSS2/color and CDS/P/2MASS/color. North is up, east is left.
The app includes each image's credits and source link in the target detail.
