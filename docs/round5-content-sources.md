# Round 5 content source checks

## Lunar observing tips

- Palus Epidemiarum: USGS/IAU feature entry (official name/position): https://planetarynames.wr.usgs.gov/Feature/4565 . Regional descriptions place Ramsden on the western stretch, with Capuanus near the southern edge: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramsden_(crater) and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palus_Epidemiarum . Bullialdus is associated with Mare Nubium, so the previous “western part” claim was removed: https://www.visit-the-moon.com/bullialdusr13-06-18-17-50/ .
- Palus Somni: USGS/IAU: https://planetarynames.wr.usgs.gov/Feature/4567 . It lies on the northeastern edge of Mare Tranquillitatis, by Sinus Concordiae and toward Mare Crisium: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinus_Concordiae and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palus_Somni .
- Sinus Medii: USGS/IAU: https://planetarynames.wr.usgs.gov/Feature/5567 . Its west side joins Mare Insularum and Mare Vaporum is to its north: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinus_Medii . Mare Nubium lies farther southwest and was removed as a defining adjacent sea.
- Sinus Roris: USGS/IAU: https://planetarynames.wr.usgs.gov/Feature/5569 . It is the northern/northwestern extension of Oceanus Procellarum and meets Mare Frigoris to the east: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinus_Roris . The tip was adjusted to describe this relation, rather than calling it simply the northwestern edge of Mare Imbrium.

## Finder coordinates

- M103 is NGC 581. SIMBAD record: https://simbad.cds.unistra.fr/simbad/sim-id?Ident=M+103 . Its J2000 position 01:33:21.4, +60:39:32 converts to 23.33917°, +60.65889°. SIMBAD quality code E means uncertainty of at least 10 arcseconds. The previous value +60:42:42 was not this catalog center.
- NGC 884 / h Persei SIMBAD: https://simbad.u-strasbg.fr/simbad/sim-id?Ident=NGC+884 . J2000 02:22:20.2, +57:08:56 converts to 35.58417°, +57.14889°. SIMBAD quality E means uncertainty of at least 10 arcseconds.
- M42 SIMBAD: https://simbad.cds.unistra.fr/simbad/sim-id?Ident=M42 . Its current listed coordinate is 05:35:16.8, −05:23:15, explicitly tagged X-ray, with a 5000 mas (5 arcsecond) error ellipse and quality D. The finder value 83.8220833°, −5.3911111° converts to 05:35:17.3, −05:23:28, about 15 arcseconds from that SIMBAD X-ray coordinate. This does not establish an error in the finder marker: M42 is an extended H II region, and its SIMBAD primary point is a low-precision X-ray position, not a definitive optical nebula centroid. Do not describe the X-ray coordinate as the nebula center. SIMBAD explains coordinate wavelength and quality fields on the same page.
