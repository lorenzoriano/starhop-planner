## Rules that follow

4. **Say the verdict on every stop.** When a theme's question is "is this X or Y?", every card states plainly which it is
   for this object (and how we know), in the first sentences, not implied.
5. **The last card answers the driving question** in words the reader could repeat.
6. **Every stop is something the reader sees in their own optics.** An object whose point is invisible in binoculars
   (an unsplittable eclipsing pair) is not a stop, however good its story; its story can live as a sentence on a
   stop that is visible.
