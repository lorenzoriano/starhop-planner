## What to fix

**A7 records does not flow.**

> "I think "right" tells a little more of a coherent story. I like "left" story, just don't think it flows. Kind of
> jumps around the five cards"

The storyline is a ladder, but the cards read as five separate records (distance, then heat, then power, power again,
then size). The transitions say "the records move to heat", which is a list moving on, not a story. Each stop must
answer something the previous stop could not, and each card must hand the reader the next question. One stop per rung
reads better than two stops on the same rung.

**A9 doubles is a collection, not a story.**

> "It's just a list of double stars, right, which is okay. It's not really a story. It's a collection. It's nice. I
> don't want that to be removed, but it just lacks a story that connects double stars."

Keep the theme. Give it one driving thread that every card serves. The strongest one the science offers: a pair in
orbit is the only scale for weighing a star, and every split on the night is a step toward using it (split it, tell
the kinds apart, rule out strangers in the line of sight, prove the partners, then find the crowds). Most A9 cards have
no theme story box at all (16 Cygni, the Double-Double and Albireo among them), and the journey skipped the
"strangers" step, which is the twist that makes the rest matter.

**A6 look-alikes: a galaxy at the end of a star story.**

> "I think right is better, but I don't understand why Andromeda is there. Talking about stars here, right?
> Andromeda's Galaxy. I don't know. I can see your point. It just doesn't seem like a strong point."

A6 is about reading two clues in starlight, brightness and color. It should stay with stars and the things stars make
(families, star cities, nurseries, a dying star's shell). Galaxies leave the A6 lens.

## Rules that follow

1. **Every card hands off.** The answer or story of each stop ends on what it could not settle, and the next stop's
   "why" picks that up. Transitions name the reason for the move, never "the records move to X".
2. **Every lens object carries a theme story box** (`story`), written for that theme's thread.
3. **A theme stays inside its subject.** A stop has to be an instance of what the theme is about, not a neighbor of it.
